# YouTube playbook

The engine for most people. The only big platform where people search with intent, so videos keep getting found long after they go live.

---

## Why it's the default engine

- **Search traffic compounds.** A good video can bring the right people for years.
- **Long-form builds trust fast.** 15 minutes with someone's thinking beats 50 short posts.
- **Everything else can be cut from it**: newsletter, text posts, clips, threads.

## The format

- **10–20 minutes**, screen share or simple talking head. Let the content set the length.
- **Low production bar.** One take, light edit (cut long pauses). The moment it becomes a production, it stops shipping weekly.
- **One topic, one real example.** Always show something real on screen.

## Title for the search, not the subscriber

The same video can find peers or buyers depending on the title. Write titles in the words the buyer types when the problem hurts (see the search phrases in `02-content-gps.md`).

- Peer-facing: "How [company] designed their onboarding"
- Buyer-facing: "Why users sign up for your app and never come back"

Same video. Different audience.

## TTH: Title, Thumbnail, Hook

- **Title:** under ~60 characters, specific, a clear payoff. Never promise what the video doesn't deliver.
- **Thumbnail:** one strong visual, 0–4 words, adds to the title rather than repeating it. Contrast (before/after, good/bad) works well.
- **Hook (first 30s):** what's at stake for the viewer, straight away. No "hey guys", no intro, no channel branding.

## Outline template

1. Hook (0–30s)
2. What we're looking at, and why it's worth studying
3. 3–5 points: what it is → why it works → what it costs you not to do it
4. What to change this week
5. CTA: lead magnet first, next step second

Bullet points, not a script.

## After publishing

- Description: 2–3 lines on the video, lead magnet link, next-step link, chapters.
- Pinned comment: the lead magnet.
- Check the audience after a few videos (YouTube Studio → Audience). If views come from the wrong people, fix titles before production.
- If a video underperforms after a week, try a new title or thumbnail before giving up on the topic.

## Shorts

Shorts mostly run on a separate feed and rarely feed long-form. Skip them unless the goal is pure reach. The search engine is the asset.
