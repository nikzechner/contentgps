# LinkedIn playbook

The pipeline channel for B2B. Buyers are here. Treat it as where deals start, not as broadcast reach.

*Algorithm notes researched in late 2026. Platforms change often, so verify any number before quoting it in a post.*

---

## How the feed works now

- **Interest graph, not network.** An AI model reads each post and decides who it's relevant to, connected or not. Topic consistency matters more than follower count.
- **The profile is part of the ranking.** Headline and About should say what the posts say. If posts and profile don't match, reach drops.
- **Generic gets buried.** Polished, generic "AI slop" posts get demoted. Patterns flagged include "it's not X, it's Y" lines, emoji bullet lists and canned openers.
- Organic reach is down a lot year over year. Posts that still travel get saved, sent and commented on properly.

## What gets rewarded (roughly by weight)

1. Saves and sends.
2. Dwell time (people staying on the post; carousels and longer stories help).
3. Real comments. Thoughtful ones count far more than "great post".
4. Early velocity in the first 60–90 minutes.

## What hurts

- External links in the post body. Put links in the first comment.
- Hashtags (slightly worse than none).
- Engagement bait ("comment YES and I'll send it").
- Polls.
- Posting in the evening.

## Formats, best to worst

| Format | Use for |
|---|---|
| Document carousel (PDF, 8–10 slides) | Studies, frameworks |
| Image + text | Before/afters, rebuilds |
| Native video (60–90s, subtitles) | Clips from the pillar piece |
| Text only | Verdicts, stories (needs a strong first two lines) |
| Article / LinkedIn newsletter | Long-form; links allowed inside |

## Post template (150–250 words)

1. **Hook (lines 1–2).** Shows before "see more". A claim, a number or a tension. No setup.
2. **The situation.** 2–3 short lines.
3. **The observation.** The thing only they would notice. This is what gets saved.
4. **What it means for the reader.** The reader is the hero.
5. **One-line CTA, or none.** Link in the first comment.

Short paragraphs, 0–1 emoji, no hashtags.

## Hook patterns

The verdict · the specific number · the buyer's side of the table · the uncomfortable truth · the confession · study one stage up.

Dead: "I'm humbled to announce", "Here's what nobody tells you", "Stop doing X, start doing Y", "Agree?", emoji openers, "Let that sink in".

## Cadence and routine

- 3–5 posts a week, weekdays. Strongest post Tue–Thu.
- Post in the morning in the buyers' time zone.
- 10–15 min before posting: comment properly on 3–5 ICP posts.
- First 60 min after: reply to every comment.

## Specificity is the moat

A real number, a real screen, a real verdict. If any person in the field could have written it, it gets demoted.
