## Standing prompts

> Paste these into Claude each week.

> Using this week's transcript and my tone file, give me 5 hooks for [platform] from the strongest observation. One line each, no setup.

> Write the newsletter from this transcript using my newsletter template. My voice, not marketing copy.

> Give me 10 titles for this piece that my ICP would search. Not peer language.

> Cut this into [n] X posts and [n] Threads posts. Rewrite per platform, never the same text twice.

> [your own]

Always rewrite the output. A draft is not a post.

---

## Rules

- Templates don't change. Ideas do.
- Never make from scratch what could be a cut.
- The newsletter and the pillar piece are the same argument.
- If the pillar piece slips, the week slips.
