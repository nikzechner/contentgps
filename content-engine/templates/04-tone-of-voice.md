## AI patterns to ban

> Defaults below. Keep, cut or add.

- Em dashes (—)
- "It's not X, it's Y" / "They don't X. They Y."
- Rhythmic triples ("faster, smarter, better")
- Neat quotable punchlines at the end of every paragraph
- Canned openers: "Let's dive in", "Here's the thing"
- Words: leverage, utilize, unlock, elevate, game-changer, delve, robust, seamless
- Emoji bullet lists
- Over-hedging
