# Threads playbook

The conversation channel. Friendlier and more personal than X. Reach comes from replies, not broadcasting.

*Algorithm notes researched in late 2026. Verify features and numbers before relying on them.*

---

## How it works now

- **Communities** are topic spaces, like subreddits. Find the ones the audience is in.
- **One topic tag per post.** Tags categorize, they don't boost reach. Put searchable words in the post itself.
- **Long text attachments** (thousands of characters) can be added to a post. Good for the full newsletter or study.
- **Engagement bait** is being pushed down. Ask real questions.

## What gets rewarded

1. **Replies.** By far the strongest signal.
2. **The user's own replies.** Replying to others is roughly as valuable as posting. Reply more than you post.
3. Early velocity in the first 30–60 minutes.
4. Relationship strength (replies from people who often talk to you count more).
5. Topic consistency (2–3 clear topics).

## What hurts

- Links in the post (put them in a reply or skip).
- Polished brand voice. Threads rewards an "inside voice".
- Cross-posting from Instagram or X without rewriting.
- One-liner hot takes with no conversation in them.

## Formats, best to worst

1. Image + text (before/afters).
2. Carousels (several images).
3. Text with a real question.
4. Post with a long text attachment.
5. Polls (light use only).

## Writing

- 500 character limit. Most good posts are 2–5 short lines.
- Structure: the observation (one line) → the take (1–2 lines, personal) → a question people can answer from their own experience.
- The question is the CTA. Ask what people know, not what they think of you.
- The most casual register of all platforms. Lowercase fine.

## Hook patterns

The honest admission · observation + question · work in progress ("here's the before, what would you change?") · a small story · a personal opinion.

Dead: "Unpopular opinion", "Drop a 🔥 if you agree", "Follow for more", the X version pasted unchanged.

## Cadence and routine

- 1–2 posts a day. Early on, consistency beats any single post.
- Before posting: 10–15 min of real replies in the niche's communities.
- First 30–60 min after: reply to every reply, ask a follow-up.
- Give it ~12 weeks before judging.

## Threads vs X

| | X | Threads |
|---|---|---|
| Energy | Sharp verdict | Warm conversation |
| Ending | Stop after the point | End with a real question |
| Best format | Video, long posts | Images, questions |
| Growth lever | Posts people share | Replies, theirs and yours |

Same idea, rewritten. Never paste the same text to both.
