# X playbook

The reach channel. Fast, opinionated, short. Builders, technical people, creators. Best for craft and taste content.

*Algorithm notes researched in late 2026. X has published its ranking weights; check the current version before quoting numbers.*

---

## How the feed works now

- **The model reads the words.** Hashtags don't help discovery. Clear, specific language does.
- **Topic consistency is an account-level asset.** Stay on 2–3 topics and make the bio match.
- **Tone is scored.** Constructive beats angry. Mutes and reports cost far more than likes earn.

## What's worth what (published weights, late 2026)

- Shares via copied link and replies between mutual follows are among the strongest signals.
- Replies, quotes and shares by DM are strong.
- Reposts and likes are weak.
- Mutes and reports are heavily negative. One report can wipe out hundreds of likes.

So: write things people want to **send** to someone, and things they want to **answer**. Spicy is fine, mean is expensive. Follow back people who engage, mutual replies get a boost.

## Rules that cost reach

- **Back-to-back posts get discounted.** Space posts several hours apart. A thread counts as one unit.
- **Premium** accounts get far more reach than free ones in third-party studies. Worth it if X is a serious channel.
- **Links:** put them in the first reply, not the main post, until the user's own data says otherwise.
- **Reply quality is scored.** A few substantial replies under bigger accounts beat many "so true" replies.

## Formats, best to worst

1. Native video (30–90s screen recordings).
2. Image + short text (before/afters, a screenshot with a verdict).
3. Long post (Premium).
4. Thread (one observation per post).
5. Text only (cheap daily volume).

## Writing

- Under 280 characters for most posts. One idea.
- Short post: the claim → optional one line of why → stop. No CTA, no hashtags, no "thoughts?".
- Thread or long post: a hook that works alone → one observation per post with a screenshot → takeaway, link in a reply.
- Lowercase is fine. Sharper than LinkedIn: fewer words, more verdict.

## Hook patterns

The verdict · the number · the test ("did X, then did Y, here's the difference") · the uncomfortable truth · the small observation · a question with a real answer.

Dead: "🧵 A thread on…", "Unpopular opinion:", "Nobody is talking about this", "This is huge 👇", hashtags.

## Cadence and routine

- 1–2 posts a day, spaced 4–5 hours apart.
- 15 min a day replying under bigger accounts in the niche.
- First 30 min after posting: reply to everyone who replies.
