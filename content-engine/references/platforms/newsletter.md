# Newsletter playbook

The owned audience. No algorithm between the user and their readers. Either the engine itself (for writers) or the partner to a YouTube engine (same topic, same day).

---

## Why it matters

- Every subscriber is reachable forever, whatever happens to social platforms.
- It reaches people when they're ready to buy, not when an algorithm decides.
- Easiest engine to start: writing only.

## Tools

Kit, Beehiiv and Substack are the common choices. Pick one and move on. If the user's tool has a Claude connector, it can be used for drafts.

## Cadence

- **Weekly, same day every week.** Consistency is the whole game early on.
- If YouTube is the engine: same topic as the video, same day.

## Issue template

1. **One thing I saw** – a real example.
2. **One thing I learned** – the insight, and why it matters to the reader.
3. **What to do with it** – one concrete action.
4. **One question** – answerable in one sentence, about the reader's own experience.
5. **Links** – the pillar piece (if any), then the next step. Same place every issue.

## Writing rules

- Get to the point in the first line. No long intros.
- One idea per section. Short paragraphs, often 1–2 sentences.
- Simple section headers, 2–5 words.
- Bold sparingly. Lists short (2–4 items).
- Subject line: specific and honest. Preview text adds to it.

## Fixing a 0% reply rate

End every issue with one easy question and **reply personally to every answer**. A reply from the right person is worth more than a hundred opens. Replies also help deliverability.

## Metrics that matter

Replies, clicks on the next step, subscribers from the lead magnet. Open rates are a weak signal now (privacy features inflate them).
