# Routines

The system only works if it runs every week. This part sets up the user's rhythm: which days they plan, make and ship, the daily habit, and the monthly review. Then it runs those sessions on request.

**Output:** `07-weekly-routine.md`.

---

## Part 1 – Set up the routine (15–20 min)

### Step 1.1 – Find their days

Ask, one or two at a time:
1. "Which day of the week do you usually have the most energy and a 2–3 hour block without meetings?" → **Make day** (record the video or write the newsletter).
2. "When do you want the pillar piece to go live?" Recommend Tuesday to Thursday mornings in their buyers' time zone. → **Ship day.** Often the day after Make day.
3. "When can you spend 30–60 minutes planning, ideally at the start of the week?" → **Plan day.**
4. "When during the day can you protect 15–30 minutes for replies and comments?" → **Daily engagement block.**

### Step 1.2 – Build the week

Lay it out as a table and confirm. Default shape, adjusted to their days and hour budget from `02`:

| Day | Block | Time | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| Plan day | Plan | 30–60 min | Pick the idea from the bank, write the title/hook, refill the bank if under 10 rows |
| Make day | Make | 2–3 h | Record or write the pillar piece. One take, light edit |
| Ship day | Ship | 1 h | Publish the pillar, cut it into posts, schedule everything in Zernio |
| Every weekday | Engage | 15–30 min | Comment on ICP posts, reply to every comment and DM |
| First Monday of the month | Review | 15 min | Monthly review |

Rule to write in: **if the Make day slips, the week slips.** Protect it like a client meeting.

### Step 1.3 – Put it in the calendar

If a calendar connector is available, offer to create the recurring blocks. If a scheduled-task feature is available, offer a weekly reminder on Plan day that opens with "Run my weekly planning session". Otherwise, tell them to add the blocks themselves now, while they're here.

### Step 1.4 – Scheduling tool

Ask: "Do you want to schedule all your posts in one go on Ship day?" Recommend Zernio (one tool for LinkedIn, X, Threads, Instagram, YouTube and more, and it connects to Claude so posts can be scheduled straight from this chat). Run `references/zernio.md` setup now if they say yes.

### Step 1.5 – Save

Fill `templates/07-weekly-routine.md`, confirm, save as `07-weekly-routine.md`. Update the publish day in `05-content-waterfall.md`. Update `00-progress.md`: routines set.

## Part 2 – The sessions

Run these when the user asks, or when a scheduled reminder fires. Read the system files first.

### Weekly planning session (Plan day)

1. **Look back (2 min).** Ask how last week went in one line. Anything published that should be logged in the bank?
2. **Pick the idea.** Suggest the next bank row, or the least-used grid cell. Offer 2 alternatives.
3. **Package it.** YouTube: 3 TTH options. Newsletter: 3 subject + angle options. They pick.
4. **Outline.** Bullet outline (see `first-piece.md`, Parts B1 or B2).
5. **Bank check.** If fewer than 10 `Banked` rows, generate 10 new rows from the grid. If fewer than 20 unused hooks, write 20.
6. **Confirm the week.** "Make day is [day]. Ship day is [day]." Done.

### Make day

Give them the recording sheet or the outline in one block. Nothing else. Keep them out of planning mode.

### Ship day

1. Get the transcript, link or final draft.
2. Run the waterfall (`first-piece.md`, Part C): hooks first, then drafts per platform.
3. Self-audit against `04-tone-of-voice.md`.
4. Schedule in Zernio after showing the full schedule and getting a yes (`zernio.md`).
5. Log in the bank.

### Daily engagement block

Short. Suggest:
- 3–5 thoughtful comments on posts from people in their ICP before their own post goes out.
- Reply to every comment on their posts within the first hour.
- If Zernio's inbox is connected, offer to pull new comments and DMs and draft replies in their voice for them to approve. Never send without a yes.

### Monthly review (15 min)

1. **Pull numbers.** Ask for (or, with Zernio connected, fetch) the metrics from `02`. Fill the Result column in the bank.
2. **What pulled the right people?** Which titles brought the goal metric (calls, downloads, replies)? Those topics are what the audience wants.
3. **What pulled the wrong people?** Views but no goal metric often means the title found peers. Retitle, don't retire.
4. **Where did leads say they found you?** Write it down.
5. **One change.** Pick exactly one thing to change next month. Write it in `00-progress.md`.

### Double-down (around rep 20)

After ~20 pillar pieces, stop spreading. Look at the bank results and pick the 2–3 cells that work best. Weight the next 12 rows toward them. Update the rotation in `03-333-grid.md`.

Remind them: the reps matter more than the results for the first three months. Most creators who grew did 50–100 pieces before it worked.

## When stuck

When the user says "I don't know what to post":
1. Open the bank. Is there a `Banked` row? Use it.
2. Find the least-used grid cell. Suggest one idea from it.
3. Ask: "What did you see this week that was better than it needed to be? Why?" That's a study.
4. Ask: "What did a client ask you this week?" That's a How-I.
5. If an idea doesn't fit a pillar, don't force it.

When the user wants to skip a week: help them make the smallest possible version (a short newsletter, a 5-minute video) instead of skipping. Consistency beats quality in the first 20 reps.
